J U D G M E N T

1. This appeal is directed against the judgment and order dated 15.01.2025 passed by the High Court of Punjab and Haryana at Chandigarh, whereby the High Court has quashed the proceedings initiated against the respondents under the Prevention of Corruption Act.

2. The core legal issue before us pertains to the interpretation of the right to speedy trial enshrined under Article 21 of the Constitution of India in the context of complex economic offences where investigation is voluminous.

3. The factual matrix, shorn of unnecessary details, reveals that the FIR was registered on 10.04.2015. Over a decade has passed without charges being framed...

"The constitutional guarantee of speedy trial is an integral part of the fundamental right to life and liberty. However, what constitutes 'delay' must be evaluated on a case-to-case basis, factoring in the systemic complexities of prosecuting economic offences."

4. We have heard the learned Additional Solicitor General for the Appellant State, and the learned Senior Counsel appearing on behalf of the respondents.

5. Consequent to our analysis, we uphold the decision of the High Court. The appeal is accordingly dismissed. Pending applications, if any, shall stand disposed of.
